ABOUT FINADVISOR

Investment intelligence with restraint.

FinAdvisor is an adaptive machine-learning dashboard for mutual-fund and investment datasets. It discovers the incoming schema, cleans data, trains a risk model, saves analytical visuals, and makes every prediction interpretable.

Predictions are educational analysis, not personal financial advice. Consider your objectives, liquidity needs, and risk tolerance before investing.
